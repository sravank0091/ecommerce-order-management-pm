
# RACI Matrix – E-commerce Order Management

**Project:** E-commerce Order Management System  
**Document:** Roles and Responsibilities  
**Status:** Illustrative portfolio case study  
**Prepared by:** Sravan Jadala, IT Project Manager

> Note: This is a simulated portfolio example with fictional project information. It does not represent confidential employer documentation.

## 1. Purpose

The RACI matrix defines roles and responsibilities for key project activities. It helps establish accountability, improve communication, and avoid confusion during project delivery.

RACI stands for:

- **R – Responsible:** Performs the work.
- **A – Accountable:** Owns the outcome and provides final approval.
- **C – Consulted:** Provides input or expertise.
- **I – Informed:** Receives updates on progress or decisions.

Each activity should have one clearly designated accountable role.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|

| Project Manager | Coordinates delivery, schedule, risks, resources, and communication. |
| Business Lead | Owns business requirements and business acceptance. |
| Business Analyst | Documents requirements, workflows, and acceptance criteria. |
| Technical Lead | Oversees technical design, architecture, and integration. |
| Development Team | Builds and configures application functionality. |
| QA Lead | Coordinates testing, defects, and quality reporting. |
| Business Users | Participate in UAT and validate business workflows. |
| Release Manager | Coordinates deployment, cutover, and release readiness. |

## 4. Governance and Accountability

- The Project Manager maintains the project schedule, RAID log, status reports, and stakeholder communication.
- The Project Sponsor provides decisions on major scope, budget, and delivery escalations.
- The Business Lead approves business requirements and confirms business acceptance.
- The Technical Lead coordinates technical delivery and resolves architecture and integration concerns.
- The QA Lead coordinates test execution, defect tracking, and quality reporting.
- The Release Manager coordinates deployment activities and production release readiness.

## 5. Communication and Escalation

| Communication | Frequency | Participants | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project status meeting | Weekly | Project Manager and workstream leads | Project Manager |
| Technical working session | As required | Technical Lead and delivery teams | Technical Lead |
| Risk and issue review | Weekly | Project Manager and relevant owners | Project Manager |
| Steering committee review | Monthly or as required | Sponsor and project leadership | Project Manager |
| Go-live readiness review | Before deployment | Project and business stakeholders | Release Manager |

## 6. Expected Outcome

The RACI matrix establishes clear ownership, supports cross-functional coordination, and helps stakeholders understand their responsibilities throughout the project lifecycle.
