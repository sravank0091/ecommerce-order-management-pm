# E-commerce Order Management System

## 1. Project Overview

This is a simulated IT project management case study demonstrating the planning and delivery of an e-commerce order management system.

The project focuses on improving order processing, inventory visibility, system integration, and customer order tracking.

**Project Type:** IT / Digital Transformation  
**Delivery Method:** Agile / Hybrid  
**Project Status:** Planning

## 2. Business Objectives

- Improve the efficiency of order processing.
- Provide better visibility into inventory availability.
- Improve integration between e-commerce, inventory, and fulfillment systems.
- Reduce manual intervention in order management.
- Improve the customer order tracking experience.

## 3. Project Scope

### In Scope

- Business requirements gathering.
- Order management workflow design.
- API and system integration planning.
- SIT and UAT coordination.
- Release planning and stakeholder communication.

### Out of Scope

- Replacement of the entire e-commerce platform.
- Physical warehouse construction.
- Third-party vendor infrastructure redesign.

## 4. Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Project Manager | Overall planning, governance, and delivery |
| Product Owner | Business priorities and requirements |
| Development Team | Application development and integration |
| QA Team | Testing and defect management |
| Business Users | UAT and business sign-off |

## 5. High-Level Milestones

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Requirements Gathering | Weeks 1-2 |
| Solution and Sprint Planning | Weeks 3-4 |
| Development and Integration | Weeks 5-10 |
| SIT and UAT | Weeks 11-12 |
| Release and Hypercare | Weeks 13-14 |

## 6. Risks and Assumptions

- Integration dependencies may affect the delivery timeline.
- Changes in business requirements may affect scope.
- Timely stakeholder participation is required for UAT.
- Test environments and required data will be available before testing.

## 7. Success Criteria

- Approved business requirements.
- Completion of planned testing and resolution of critical defects.
- Business stakeholder sign-off.
- Successful deployment and transition to hypercare.

## 8. Disclaimer

This is a fictional portfolio case study created to demonstrate IT project management capabilities. It does not represent an actual Walmart, JPMorgan Chase, or Cognizant project, and all project details are illustrative.
