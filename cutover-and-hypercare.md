## 2. Objectives

- Coordinate a controlled transition from the test environment to production.
- Confirm business and technical readiness before deployment.
- Minimize disruption to order processing and customer experience.
- Establish support and escalation procedures after go-live.

## 5. Go/No-Go Decision

### Go Criteria

- Required business and technical approvals are obtained.
- Critical business workflows pass production smoke testing.
- No unresolved critical defects or unapproved high-impact risks remain.
- Support coverage and rollback procedures are confirmed.

### No-Go Criteria

- Critical order-processing functionality fails.
- A major production dependency is unavailable.
- Required approvals are missing.
- The technical team cannot execute the approved rollback procedure.

The final decision will be made by authorized business and technology stakeholders.

## 6. Rollback Plan

If a critical issue occurs after deployment:

1. The technical lead assesses the issue and informs the project manager.
2. The incident is documented and escalated according to severity.
3. Authorized stakeholders decide whether rollback is required.
4. The technical team executes the approved rollback procedure.
5. QA validates restoration of critical workflows.
6. Stakeholders receive a status update and recovery confirmation.

**Rollback owner:** Technical Lead  
**Decision authority:** Designated business and technology stakeholders  
**Rollback steps:** To be validated with the technical team before deployment.

## 7. Hypercare Support Plan

**Illustrative duration:** 10 business days following go-live.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Project Manager | Coordinates daily reviews, issues, and stakeholder updates |
| Technical Lead | Coordinates technical investigation and resolution |
| QA Team | Performs validation and regression testing |
| Business Support | Reports operational issues and validates business outcomes |
| Product Owner | Prioritizes business-impacting issues |

## 8. Issue Severity and Escalation

| Severity | Example | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Order processing unavailable | Immediate escalation to technical and business leads |
| High | Major payment or inventory workflow impaired | Prioritized investigation and stakeholder updates |
| Medium | Partial functionality issue with workaround | Track and resolve through agreed support process |
| Low | Minor usability or display issue | Record and prioritize for planned resolution |

Response and resolution targets will be agreed upon before go-live.

## 9. Hypercare Exit Criteria

Hypercare may close when:

- Critical incidents are resolved.
- High-priority issues have approved resolution plans.
- Business stakeholders confirm operational stability.
- Open issues have assigned owners and target dates.
- Support ownership is formally transitioned to the operations team.
